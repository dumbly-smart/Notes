# How this second brain works

## The workflow

1. **Capture:** create a note. New notes automatically land in [[00 Inbox/00 Inbox|Inbox]].
2. **Clarify:** give it one useful title and state why it matters.
3. **Connect:** add at least one `[[link]]` to a project, area, or map.
4. **Organize:** move it only when its destination is obvious.
5. **Review:** use [[Home]] daily and [[Atlas]] weekly.

## Where things go

- `00 Inbox` — unprocessed notes and fast capture.
- `01 Daily` — daily logs, observations, and decisions.
- `02 Projects` — outcomes with a finish line.
- `03 Areas` — ongoing responsibilities and skills.
- `Hacking` — your security knowledge library.
- `99 System` — templates, attachments, and system documentation.

## Automatic mapping

Folder notes containing `%% Waypoint %%` are regenerated whenever notes are created, renamed, moved, or deleted. [[Atlas]] and [[Home]] use Dataview queries, so their tables rebuild from the current vault state. You maintain the notes; the maps maintain themselves.

## Daily rhythm

- **Start:** open [[Home]], then today's daily note.
- **During the day:** capture quickly; do not interrupt the thought to classify it.
- **End:** clear actionable items from the Inbox and add links.
- **Weekly:** review active projects and the orphaned-notes section in [[Atlas]].

## Useful commands

- `Create new note` — instant Inbox capture.
- `Open today's daily note` — timeline and journal.
- `Omnisearch: Search in vault` — fast full-text retrieval.
- `Tasks: Create or edit task` — dates, recurrence, and priorities.
- `QuickAdd: Run QuickAdd` — extensible capture menu.
- `Homepage: Open homepage` — return to the dashboard.
