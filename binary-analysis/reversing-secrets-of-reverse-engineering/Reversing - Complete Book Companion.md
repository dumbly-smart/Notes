# Reversing: Secrets of Reverse Engineering — Complete Book Companion

> [!important] What this is
> These are chapter-by-chapter companion notes for the actual 625-page book—not a reading schedule. They preserve the book’s order, reconstruct its major examples and case studies, explain difficult IA-32/Windows concepts, and clearly label modern additions.

> [!warning] Historical, legal, and safety context
> The book was published in 2005. Exact Windows internals, tools, malware, protection systems, and legal examples are historical. Verify modern platform behavior and applicable law independently. Malware and protection-analysis work belongs only in isolated, authorized labs; added exercises use benign targets.

## How mastery is measured

Reading creates familiarity. Mastery requires that you can explain, predict, reconstruct, test, and document unfamiliar programs. Each chapter ends with explanation and solving checklists; the final labs require independent evidence rather than copied walkthrough steps.
